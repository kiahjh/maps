# Cataract detail study

This is a local development sample, not a completed realistic environment or a surveyed map. One local world unit is one metre. Regional positions still come from the schematic and require a separate reconstruction.

## Visual direction

The accepted direction is a simple, beautiful landscape built from project-authored geometry and procedural materials. Preserve the cliff texture and sculpted ledges, with extra recesses on either side of the falls. Water, shrubs and grass should enrich this style without turning the atlas into a photorealistic asset scene. The live atlas and reusable model exports use no downloaded models or texture images; the Blender study is paused.

## Textual constraints

[Chapter XVI](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1640/1640-h/1640-h.htm#link2HCH0016): Vane reaches a rugged rocky ascent after crossing desert; moss grows at its foot. He climbs the ancient cataract. Sand has filled the upper river course. The former river has two branches, with Evil Wood between them on the far horizon.

[Chapter XLV](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1640/1640-h/1640-h.htm#link2HCH0045): the restored river roars, swirls and drops sheer, dividing into two branches. The party follows its right bank and climbs the height. Above, a broad, deep, quiet, brim-full river lies between grassy banks, with forests and flowering shrubs further upstream.

## Provisional dimensions and invented forms

- Water drop: 38 m. Upper channel: 40 m wide. Lower channels taper to 22 m and 26 m. These are design choices, not measurements in the novel.
- Inspection figure: approximately 1.8 m tall, selectable in the compass menu. It is a neutral size reference, not a portrayal of Vane.
- The ascent rises about 38 m over approximately 150 m of horizontal travel. Its exact position, route and ledges are invented.
- Rock geology, leaf forms, shrub flowers, detailed erosion and plant locations are interpretations. No species or specific rock formation is claimed to come from the text.
- Local green vegetation is shown in Water mode; rocks and the moss at the foot remain in Dry mode.
- Six fallen stream boulders are partly buried in the bed below the falls, with animated bow foam and downstream wakes. Smaller shrubs root on gentler rock shelves; bank thickets and patches of ivory, muted gold and lilac flowers follow the existing terrain. These individual placements and colours are artistic interpretations.

## Current limits

Terrain uses a refined continuous height field: it can form ledges and broken slopes but cannot create proper overhangs or caves. Cliff colour and surface relief are generated in world space. Water uses connected animated folds, river currents, foam and spray, not a fluid simulation. Trees are project-authored prototypes. The outer landscape still uses older simplified trees and terrain. Broad regional travel distances are not yet calibrated.

The study must be judged in the browser from front, side, close and dry views. Numeric checks guard scale and channel clearance; they do not establish visual quality.
